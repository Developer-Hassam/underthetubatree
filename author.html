Meet Tuba Seyal

Author. Storyteller. Dreamer.

Tuba Seyal is the author behind Under The Tuba Tree, a little world created from a love of stories, meaningful connections, and the magic of childhood.

Through her writing, Tuba hopes to create stories that reach beyond the pages, stories that nurture imagination, celebrate cultural roots, explore faith, and offer comfort to little hearts.

From heartfelt stories like Whispers from Jannah to the adventures taking root in the Little Roots collection, she believes every child deserves stories that make them feel seen, connected, and inspired. Welcome beneath the tree.
