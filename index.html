Pakistani Stories Collection

Little Roots

Little stories. Big roots. A lifetime of memories.

What does growing up Pakistani feel like?

It's endless cousin adventures, summer afternoons beneath mango trees, family gatherings filled with laughter, familiar traditions and whispered stories that make bedtime a little more magical.

Little Roots is a growing collection celebrating the beauty of Pakistani childhood through stories filled with culture, family, imagination and faith.

The collection may explore:

  • Pakistani childhood memories
  • Cousin adventures
  • Family traditions
  • Imaginative jinn tales
  • Cultural celebrations
  • Islamic values woven into everyday stories

Meet Tuba Seyal

Author. Storyteller. Dreamer.

Welcome beneath my tree!

I'm Tuba Seyal, the author behind Under The Tuba Tree, a little world created from a love of stories, meaningful connections and the magic of childhood.

My hope is to create stories that reach beyond the pages, stories that nurture imagination, celebrate cultural roots, explore faith and offer comfort to little hearts.

From heartfelt stories like Whispers from Jannah to the adventures taking root in Little Roots, I believe every child deserves stories that make them feel seen, connected and inspired.

Thank you for being part of this growing story.

With love, Tuba.
