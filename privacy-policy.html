Thank you for spending a little time beneath our tree. Your privacy matters to us, especially because some of our stories explore tender subjects like love and loss. This policy explains what information we collect on underthetubatree.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Under The Tuba Tree is an author brand created by Tuba Seyal, home to children's stories including Whispers from Jannah. You can reach us at hello@underthetubatree.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Newsletter signup: your email address (and your first name, if the form asks for it).
  • Contact form: your name, email address and the message you write to us.
  • Emails you send us: the content of your message and your email address.

Please share only what you are comfortable sharing. We use your messages only to read and reply to them with care.

Information collected automatically
When you visit the site, our hosting provider and tools may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the date and time of your visit. [If you use analytics, add: We use [analytics tool name] to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are most popular. This information is used in aggregate.]

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your questions and messages.
  • To send our newsletter and updates about new books and collections, only if you have chosen to subscribe.
  • To keep the website secure, working properly and easy to use.
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. Newsletter and consent

We send newsletters only to people who sign up and agree to receive them. You can unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link at the bottom of every email, or by writing to hello@underthetubatree.com. We will honor your request promptly.

Our newsletters are sent through [MailerLite / Mailchimp], which stores subscriber email addresses on our behalf.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not rent or trade our email list.

We share information only with trusted service providers who help us run the site, and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Website hosting: Hostinger.
  • Email newsletter service: [MailerLite / Mailchimp].
  • Contact form and email delivery tools used to receive your messages.
  • [Analytics provider, if used.]

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our visitors and our work.

6. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. This site may use cookies that are necessary for it to work, and [analytics cookies, if used] to help us understand how it is used. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

7. Links to other websites

Our site links to other websites, including book retailers (such as Amazon), Instagram and Facebook. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them. We encourage you to read their policies before sharing personal information.

8. Children's privacy

Our stories are for children, but this website is intended for parents, caregivers, educators and other adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us at hello@underthetubatree.com and we will delete it.

9. How long we keep your information

We keep newsletter details until you unsubscribe, and contact messages only as long as needed to reply and keep a reasonable record. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

10. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct or update it.
  • Delete it.
  • Stop sending you emails.

Depending on where you live (for example, California, the European Union or the United Kingdom), you may have additional legal rights, including the right to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email hello@underthetubatree.com. We may need to confirm your identity before we act on it.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including secure (HTTPS) connections and trusted providers. No website or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

12. International visitors

Our website is operated from the United States, and our service providers may process information in other countries. By using the site, you understand that your information may be transferred and stored outside your country.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will show when it was last changed. If we make major changes, we will post a notice on this page.
